Required format

drug1_id,drug2_id,relation_type
58,89,2
  • For indices, use the headers shown above. For names, DrugBank IDs or PubChem CIDs, use drug1,drug2,relation_type.
  • Use nonnegative integer local drug indices and distinct drugs in each row.
  • Choose a supported drug-drug relation ID from the homepage dropdown.
  • DrugBank IDs identify DrugBank records; PubChem CIDs identify compounds. Use them in the drug1/drug2 columns. Drugs outside the model graph receive an error, with no model score.

The output includes uncalibrated_model_score and error. Invalid rows receive an error and no score.

Batch scores do not establish an adverse interaction or the safety of taking two drugs together.
